WebXR · Three.js + Rapier

Put on your Quest 3 and click "Enter VR" below.

  • Right thumbstick — move sphere on X / Y axes
  • Right controller button (A/X) — toggle sphere physics ON/OFF
  • Right controller button (B/Y) — teleport into the bridge (press again to return)
  • Left thumbstick — move sphere on Z axis
  • Left trigger — accelerator multiplier for Z movement
